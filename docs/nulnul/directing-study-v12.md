## Why v11 failed

The scan was an effect without a readable decision: a spotlight swept the field, but the chosen deployment points did not become the next visual beat. The finale was worse: the UFO travelled along a top rail and lit 123 cells one by one, so the audience saw a plotter performing work instead of one authored reveal.

## Reusable directing rules

- **Stage one idea per beat.** Satoshi Kon's editing compresses an idea into one decisive visual transition; animation should contain only intended information. Here: observe, decide, deploy—never scan and decorate at the same time.
- **Use `ma` before the payoff.** Miyazaki's quiet interval keeps a film from sitting at constant intensity. The empty pasture therefore holds for 280ms before the signature return.
- **Make movement carry meaning.** Kurosawa composes weather, groups, and camera movement around the dramatic change. In a fixed SVG camera, the opening now relies on the UFO's stop and turn; the field wave remains the finale's only dominant line.
- **Previsualize the eye path.** Fortiche's *Arcane* process treats storyboard flow and shot intention as the foundation, not an effect pass. Each fixed 700px frame must have one first read.
- **VFX must explain the mechanic—but may be omitted when action already does.** The scan bar and crosshairs duplicated information and broke the pasture style, so the UFO's subsequent route carries the decision. The finale keeps only a contribution-cell crest because it uniquely explains the field-wide reveal.
- **Let the audience complete omitted action.** Comics and manga use closure and panel size to control time. The finale does not show the UFO drawing every cell; one central impact and 53 half-cell Euclidean distance phases let the viewer infer that the pasture changed.
- **Titles serve the work.** Saul Bass rejected spectacle that becomes detached from the film. `SEONARU` is the final image, so the effect disappears and the name holds cleanly.
- **Escalation changes state.** A middle incident earns attention only when the scene is different afterward. The UFO therefore performs one real relocation, then the final two sheep enter the newly opened area; it never becomes a permanent follower.

## Rejection rules

- No spotlight cone, travelling top rail, cell-by-cell plotter, thread laser, lightning, particle field, camera shake, blur, or flower decoration.
- No scan line, crosshair, drop marker, or explanatory HUD.
- The representative drop route must increase in x at every stop and alternate vertical weight; no horizontal double-back.
- No grass reaction before the responsible sheep is visibly present.
- No explicit circle or ellipse geometry, and no username cell that outruns or trails its shared Euclidean wave phase.
- No final hold shorter than 1.40 seconds.
- No visual judgement above the actual 700px GitHub presentation size.
- No continuous UFO following, repeated relocation, permanent glow trail, extra particles, or growth beyond 1.15 on the current 80% sheep base.
- No one-line signature beyond 13 characters. Use two centred 3×3 lines through 26 characters; reject longer input instead of silently truncating or collapsing it into unreadable cells.
